On the Fusion 360
Shop Talk Podcast

Vanguard runs out of a one person shop, so it's not often the work gets to leave that shop. This time it did. I sat down for a conversation on the Fusion 360 Shop Talk Podcast about how a wheel actually goes from an idea to something bolted onto a car.

It was a good conversation, and a rare chance to talk about why Vanguard exists instead of just showing off the wheels.
