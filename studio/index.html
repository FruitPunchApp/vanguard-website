Beyond the Lineup

One-Off Wheels,
Built to Spec

The Studio is where builds that don't fit the standard lineup get made. Not every car is a modern water-cooled VW, and not every idea fits neatly into BRD, Taza, or CC1 — this is the custom design track for the ones that don't.

Every Studio commission starts from a conversation about the car, the fitment, and the look you're after, then gets designed in Fusion 360 from there. Nothing off a shelf, nothing made-to-order in the traditional sense — one wheel, one build, one owner.

Design Process

From Sketch
to Spec

Custom work moves through the same design discipline as the standard lineup, just pointed at a single build instead of a catalog piece.

01
Consult
Talk through the car, the fitment constraints, and the direction you want the design to take.
02
Design
Full CAD design in Fusion 360, built around your specific fitment rather than a generic bolt pattern.
03
Review
Renders and revisions until the design is right, before anything goes into production.
04
Build
Forged and finished to spec, one set, for one car.
